    Our Story

    Transforming lives through holistic healing

    Life Changing Journey is a Durban-based wellness organisation bringing together specialist practitioners in psychology, spiritual healing, financial guidance, hypnotherapy, professional consulting, and rural education. We believe every person carries the seed of an extraordinary life — we help you find it.

    Rooted in African wisdom and guided by Ubuntu philosophy, our integrated approach honours the whole person — mind, spirit, community, and future.
